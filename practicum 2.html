Dark Mode

Theme switching with JavaScript, CSS custom properties and localStorage

Current Theme

You are currently viewing: Dark mode.

Click "Toggle Theme" in the navigation bar to switch. Your choice is saved so the same theme loads the next time you visit.

How It Works

On first visit (with no saved preference), the page checks your system's preferred color scheme using window.matchMedia('(prefers-color-scheme: dark)'). After that, your manual choice is stored in localStorage and always takes priority.
